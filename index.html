Founded by Fortune

For the People on the Streets

Founded by Fortune, the brand was created for the people on the streets — for everyone who works hard but never sees themselves reflected in luxury fashion. Blackboy Fortune exists to change that. Every piece is made so that anyone who puts in the work can afford to wear it, without compromising on quality or style.

At its core, the brand stands for resilience, struggle, and freedom — the freedom to express yourself, no matter where you're coming from. It's about bringing luxury and comfort to a community that's often priced out of it, and proving that style shouldn't be reserved for the few.

The Colours

Life Lived in Full Colour

Inspired by the culture and the "big dogs" shaping the fashion industry, Blackboy Fortune was built to match that pace — while staying true to the streets that inspired it. From the bold black-and-white foundation to pops of purple, red, and white, every color and graphic tells a story: one of ambition, hustle, and life lived in full color.

Brand StoryVol. 01 • Lagos

The First Drop: How BlackBoy Fortune Came to Life

Every brand has a beginning. Ours started on the streets of Lagos — with one simple idea: everybody deserves to look sharp.

BlackBoy Fortune didn't start in a studio or an office. It started from watching. Watching people grind, hustle, and survive every single day without ever getting a taste of what luxury feels like. The traders, the students, the dreamers — the people who put in the most work were the ones priced out of looking the part.

That didn't sit right. So the first piece was born from one simple idea: everybody deserves to look sharp. Everybody deserves to feel like somebody. And if you're willing to work, you should be able to afford it.

The first drop was small, but the message was loud — quality you can feel, style that speaks before you do, and a name that stands for freedom. From the streets of Lagos to wherever you're reading this from: welcome to the era of BlackBoy Fortune.

MissionVol. 02 • Lagos

Till the Name Is in Lights

The mission is simple: keep pushing, no matter how tough it gets, until the name is in lights. This is what that looks like from the inside.

There's a picture we hold onto — a billboard, somewhere over the Lagos traffic, with two words on it: BLACKBOY FORTUNE. That picture is the mission.

Building a brand from nothing isn't a smooth road. There are slow days, doubts, and moments where quitting looks easy. But resilience isn't a value we printed on a label — it's how the brand survives. We don't fold. We rise.

Every order answered, every piece delivered, every person who wears the logo out in Lekki, Ipaja, Ikorodu or anywhere in the world — that's another step toward the billboard. And when we get there, it won't just be a win for the brand. It'll be a win for everybody who was ever told luxury wasn't for them.

Keep pushing. The name is going up in lights. 🚀

DesignVol. 03 • Lagos

The Colours of the Hustle: Black, White, Purple & Red

Black and white is the foundation. Purple, red, and white are the sparks. Every colour on a BBF piece tells a story.

Every BlackBoy Fortune piece starts with the same foundation: black and white. Bold. Clean. Unbothered. It's the base every hustler stands on — no noise, no pretending.

Then come the pops of colour. Purple for ambition — the colour of kings built from nothing. Red for the struggle and the fire that keeps you going when the odds say stop. White for freedom — a clean slate, a fresh start, room to become whoever you're working to be.

No colour on a BlackBoy Fortune piece is random. Ambition, hustle, and life lived in full colour — that's the whole language. When you wear it, you're not just matching an outfit. You're saying something.

Style GuideVol. 04 • Lagos

Lagos Streetwear Done Right: How to Wear BBF

Cap and a clean tee. The full tracksuit. Sleeveless for the heat. Here's how the family wears it.

Streetwear in Lagos has its own rules — the heat is real, the moves are plenty, and the fit has to speak before you do. Here's how to run your BlackBoy Fortune pieces:

The Everyday Hustle: A plain BBF tee — black or white — with the logo doing the talking. Add the Signature Cap and you're set for anything the day throws at you.

The Full Statement: The tracksuit, head to toe. Purple & White if you're feeling royal. Black & Red if you came to be seen. Dark Blue & Red for the ones who move in silence.

Built for the Heat: The sleeveless shirt was made for Lagos sun and Lagos energy. Black for the night moves, white for the daytime grind.

Cool Evenings: When the breeze picks up, the collar sweater — orange themed if you're bold — layers perfectly over a tee.

However you wear it, one rule: wear it like you earned it. Because you did.
